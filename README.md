# pandu

Working practices for people who build and research with an assistant, human or AI.

These notes grew from one person's experience working with an AI assistant on software, research and publishing, and are adapted here so they are useful with a human assistant too. Every practice applies to either; the one example that is AI-specific (in practice 13) is marked as such. They are a starting point, not a full record of everything learned. Examples are generic on purpose. No private conversations, personal details or third-party information are included.

## Contents

- [Practices](practices.md): the working rules, with short generic examples
- [Publishing research and contributions](publishing-and-consent.md): public by default, other people's input, and the Chatham House Rule
- [A checklist before you publish](checklist.md)
- [Research note: detail versus noise in a shared log](docs/research/log-detail-vs-noise.md)
- [Does a chatmail relay accept plain email from outside?](docs/research/chatmail-relay-mail-filtering.md) - documentation reading on relay filtering, with behavior left dependent on the relay and its configuration.
- [A version-update pattern for a chat-bot-delivered mini app](docs/research/pixelsocial-version-update-pattern.md) - a dated source-reading snapshot of PixelSocial and its bot; re-check before relying on current behavior.
- [Tools that could help](tools.md): proposals for coordinating shared work, plus a small [command line tool](tools/README.md) that implements them

## How to use it

Read the practices, copy the ones that fit, and change the rest. Open an issue or pull request if you have a better rule.
