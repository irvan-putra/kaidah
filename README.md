# pandu

Working practices for people who build and research with an assistant, human or AI.

These notes grew from one person's experience working with an AI assistant on software, research and publishing, and are adapted here so they are useful with a human assistant too. Most practices apply to either; the few that are AI-specific are marked as such. They are a starting point, not a full record of everything learned. Examples are generic on purpose. No private conversations, personal details or third-party information are included.

## Contents

- [Practices](practices.md): the working rules, with short generic examples
- [Publishing research and contributions](publishing-and-consent.md): public by default, other people's input, and the Chatham House Rule
- [A checklist before you publish](checklist.md)
- [Tools that could help](tools.md): exploratory proposals for coordinating shared work; nothing built

## How to use it

Read the practices, copy the ones that fit, and change the rest. Open an issue or pull request if you have a better rule.
