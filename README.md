# pandu

Working practices for people who build and research with an AI assistant.

These notes come from one person's experience working with an assistant on software, research and publishing. They are a starting point, not a full record of everything learned. Examples are generic on purpose. No private conversations, personal details or third-party information are included.

## Contents

- [Practices](practices.md): the working rules, with short generic examples
- [Publishing research and contributions](publishing-and-consent.md): public by default, other people's input, and the Chatham House Rule
- [A checklist before you publish](checklist.md)

## How to use it

Read the practices, copy the ones that fit, and change the rest. Open an issue or pull request if you have a better rule.
