# kaidah

Parked project. Renamed from pandu on 2026-11-24; no active automation.

Working practices for people who build and research with an assistant, human or AI.

These notes grew from one person's experience working with an AI assistant on software, research and publishing, and are adapted here so they are useful with a human assistant too. Every practice applies to either; the one example that is AI-specific (in practice 13) is marked as such. They are a starting point, not a full record of everything learned. Examples are generic on purpose. No private conversations, personal details or third-party information are included.
